HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

Sound, not sequence

Most people meet numerology through the Pythagorean method, which runs straight down the alphabet: A=1, B=2, C=3. Chaldean numerology does something older and stranger — it groups letters by the sound and vibration they carry, so the numbers attached to each letter look scattered rather than sequential.

The three numbers this tool gives you

Destiny comes from every letter in the full name — it's read as the overall shape a name pushes a person toward.

Soul Urge uses only the vowels — the traditional reading is an inner motivation, the thing a person wants that they don't always say aloud.

Personality uses only the consonants — the impression a name gives before anyone gets to know the person behind it.

Why the number 9 never appears

In the Chaldean chart, 9 is treated as complete in itself — powerful enough that assigning it to a letter was considered a misuse of it. It can still appear as the result of adding other numbers together, but no letter is ever worth 9 on its own. You'll notice the reference table below skips straight from 8 back to 1.

BEYOND THE SINGLE DIGIT

Why the compound number matters too

Two names can reduce to the same single digit and still carry very different weight, because the compound number they came from — the number before it's added down — has its own separate meaning. A 6 built from 15 reads differently to a 6 built from 24.
